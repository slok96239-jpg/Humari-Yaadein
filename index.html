01 / 03 · EK KHOOBSURAT SHAAM

Ganga ke kinare, tum aur main ❤️

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi ke sabse khoobsurat pal kisi badi jagah ya kisi khaas mauke par nahi milte. Woh bas ek shaant si shaam mein mil jaate hain.

Chand ki roshni, Ganga ki lehron ki awaaz, jalte hue diye aur paas tumhara hona... Shayad isi ko sukoon kehte hain.

Agar waqt ko kuch der ke liye rok paata, toh main isi pal ko baar-baar jeena chahta. Kyunki jagah kitni bhi khoobsurat ho, uski khoobsurati tab badh jaati hai jab saath tumhara ho.

Ek shaam, hazaar yaadein... ❤️

02 / 03 · EK KHOOBSURAT DUA

Ek dua tumhare naam ❤️

Mandir ki ghantiyon ki awaaz aur saamne jalte diye... Is khoobsurat jagah par bas ek hi khayal aata hai.

Har kisi ki zindagi mein kuch log aise hote hain jo bina kuch kahe bhi chehre par muskaan le aate hain.

Meri dua hai ki tumhari zindagi hamesha khushiyon se bhari rahe. Tumhare har sapne ko manzil mile aur har naye din mein muskurane ki koi wajah ho.

Saamne mandir, paas tum... ❤️

03 / 03 · EK YAADGAAR KAHANI

Movie se bhi khoobsurat ❤️

Cinema hall ki lights band ho jaati hain, screen par film shuru hoti hai aur aas-paas sirf kahani ki awaaz hoti hai.

Lekin kabhi-kabhi asli kahani screen par nahi hoti. Woh un chhote-chhote palon mein hoti hai jo hum kisi apne ke saath bitaate hain.

AWARAPAN 2 ki ye yaad bhi hamari kahani ka ek khoobsurat hissa rahegi. Film khatam ho sakti hai, lekin achhi yaadein hamesha saath rehti hain.

The End? Never. ❤️
